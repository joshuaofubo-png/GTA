    Essential Online Safety Tips

    Simple habits that can help prevent financial and identity theft.

    🔐 Never Share Your OTP

    Never reveal one-time passwords, mobile-money PINs or banking credentials to callers or strangers.

    💳 Verify Before Paying

    Confirm payment requests independently using official contact information.

    🔗 Inspect Links

    Check the exact domain before entering passwords or personal details.

    🎁 Question Free Offers

    Be cautious of unexpected prizes, giveaways and investment opportunities promising guaranteed returns.

    💼 Verify Job Offers

    Be suspicious of employers demanding registration fees, deposits or payments to secure employment.

    📞 Contact Official Support

    If someone claims to represent your bank or mobile-money provider, contact the organization through its official channels.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Understand how ScamShield works.

    Is ScamShield free?

    Yes. This initial version is a free, browser-based tool.

    Can ScamShield detect every scam?

    No. It uses predefined warning patterns and cannot detect every fraudulent website, phone number or message.

    Does a safe result guarantee a website is legitimate?

    No. A website can be fraudulent even when no warning indicators are detected. Always verify independently.

    Does it check live scam databases?

    No. The initial version performs local pattern analysis. Live reputation checks require trusted external threat-intelligence services and a secure backend.

    Does it send my messages to a server?

    No. This implementation analyzes submitted content inside your browser. Recent scan history is stored locally on your device.

    What should I do if I have already been scammed?

    Contact your bank or mobile-money provider immediately through official channels, secure your accounts, preserve evidence and report the incident to the relevant authorities.
